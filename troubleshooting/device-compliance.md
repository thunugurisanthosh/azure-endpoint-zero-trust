# Troubleshooting — Device Compliance

## Symptom
WIN11-01 shows Not compliant.

## Investigation
1. Open Intune → Devices → WIN11-01.
2. Open Compliance.
3. Identify the failed requirement.
4. Check the affected security setting.
5. Remediate the endpoint.
6. Trigger device Sync.
7. Recheck compliance.

## Evidence
Record:
- Symptom
- Failed policy
- Root cause
- Remediation
- Final status
