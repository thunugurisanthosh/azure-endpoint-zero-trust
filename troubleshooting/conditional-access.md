# Troubleshooting — Conditional Access Block

## Investigation
1. Open Entra ID → Sign-in logs.
2. Select the affected sign-in.
3. Review Conditional Access results.
4. Identify the policy that caused the result.
5. Check MFA and device compliance state.
6. Remediate the test endpoint or policy.
7. Retest with the test user.

## Important
Do not publish sensitive sign-in details.
